A second look at the callsign you hear.

Logger32-SCP and HRD-SCP suggest matching and similar callsigns while you type in your logging program. Useful during everyday contacts and contests.

Compare possibilities quickly, spot a possible typing or copying error, and choose the callsign you heard.

For Logger32

Logger32-SCP

Works alongside Logger32 and receives DX Cluster spots through Logger32’s own spot feed. Select a suggestion with a click, or accept your entry with Space.

Explore Logger32-SCP
For Ham Radio Deluxe

HRD-SCP

Works with HRD Logbook’s Add a new Entry window. It can open, close and follow that window, with a separate DX Cluster connection.

Explore HRD-SCP

From a partial call to a clearer choice

01 · TYPE

Use your logger’s Call field

Suggestions start after two characters and update as you type.

02 · COMPARE

See close alternatives

Exact matches appear first, followed by similar callsigns ranked by similarity.

03 · SELECT

Keep logging

Click a suggestion to transfer it, or press Space in the logger to accept your entry.

A database match means a callsign is stored in the database. It does not prove that it is the callsign you heard.

Your database. Your display.

Import callsigns from ADIF, update the SCP database, choose light or dark colors, and use the interface in English, Greek, German, Italian, French or Dutch. Approved program updates preserve your personal callsigns and settings.

Both applications are free to download and use. A guide in all six languages is included.
